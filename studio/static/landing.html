FAQ

What is a "reference"?

Any short edit whose style you like — for example a velocity edit you saw on TikTok. The AI measures its cuts, timing, zooms, transitions and effects, then applies that structure to your clips.

Do you copy the reference video?

No. Only the editing structure is used. Your final video contains only your own footage (and the reference's audio only if you don't add music).

How long does it take?

Usually a few minutes, depending on length and quality. You can leave the page — your video keeps processing.

What if the result fails?

If a video can't be created, your credits are returned automatically.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Open the site on Android or iPhone and add it to your home screen to use it like an app.

How do I pay?

Card or PayPal. In Mozambique you can also pay with e-Mola.
